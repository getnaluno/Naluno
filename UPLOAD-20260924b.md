# Naluno 2026.09.24b

Unzip into the GitHub repo root so these files land in the folders that already exist (`app/`, `js/`, `css/`, `admin/`). Do not upload a new folder named `public`.

Deploy separately, after the Pages files:

- `firestore.rules` — required for Advertise (a creator saving an ad) and for the strict Seen-by list. Until it is published, Seen by still records a watch in your notification inbox, and Advertise will say the server refused the save.

The economy worker is unchanged (`2.6.10-play`). Do not redeploy it for this pack.

What this pack changes:

- Seen by lists people, and a reaction sticks, even when the Signal viewer rules are not published yet.
- Watching a Broadcast from a Signal closes the Signal first so playback is not fighting a second video.
- The phone Back button steps back through Naluno (close what is open, then the previous tab) instead of leaving the app. On the first screen, Back still leaves.
- Advertise, in the Broadcast ⋯ menu, opens the same kind of form as the console. Save puts a paused ad in the console. The prepaid amount opens a payment step that is not connected yet — nothing is charged.
- A saved Broadcast under Callsign opens that Broadcast.
- Ad maths uses the rate card when a unit’s own rate is zero, and a completed watch still counts if the impression did not. A zero rate card is called out in the journal.
