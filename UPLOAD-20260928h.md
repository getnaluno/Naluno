## 3. Turn off three call indexes (one-time)
Firebase console → Firestore Database → Indexes → Single field → Add

(Or, with the Firebase CLI: `firebase deploy --only firestore:indexes`.)

indexes on, Firestore caps new calls at about 500 per second.

## What was wrong (after a call)
After a call that started inside a Broadcast, the Broadcast stayed drawn
over the app: its back button, the phone's Back and the page below stopped
responding. Ending a call with the phone's Back also swallowed the next Back.

## What was wrong (found by the call stress test)
- Answering just after the caller hung up brought the call back to life:
  the answering phone sat alone in a dead call.
- Calling someone who was already on a call rang for a full minute. They
  never knew, and you were never told.
- Two people calling each other at the same moment: neither connected.
- A caller whose phone died left a ring in the database, and it rang again
  hours later with nobody there.
- An incoming call while you were on the call lobby was cut off at once.
- A call could be taken over by a second incoming call in the first moments.
- A call that never connected sat on "connecting" forever.
- Declining quickly could leave the camera on.
- Tapping Join and then Cancel while the camera was still opening: the
  other phone rang anyway with nobody on this end. A call arriving in that
  same moment was cut off, and the camera stayed on.
- A Signal left open behind a call kept playing and closed itself.

## Fixed
- Call status only moves forward (in the app and in the rules).
- A busy person's phone answers "busy" at once; you see "… is on another call".
- Crossed calls connect: one phone gives up its own call and answers the other.
- Old rings are marked missed and not shown. A ring stops by itself after 80s.
- A call that has not connected after 40s ends with "The call could not connect".
- An incoming call replacing the lobby keeps working, and the screen you were
  on comes back afterwards.
- A call cancelled or crossed while the camera opens never rings the other
  phone (or is ended the moment it was written), and no camera is left on.
- "Answered on another device" when you pick up elsewhere.
