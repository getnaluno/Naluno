What changed
- Call cameras that come out landscape are sent upright 3:4. The full height is kept and only the unused sides are trimmed, so the face is not zoomed and the other phone is not a thin strip.
- Origin no longer gives up at 8 seconds. A slow frame check cannot throw away the open-web result. No match is "clear", not "unfinished".
- iPhone video upload copies the file before the picker is cleared, and does not try to convert HEVC on the phone.
- "Sign in required for calls" is not shown when Firebase already has you. A failed call-wake is not announced as "no connection" while the call is connecting.
- Each call rings on its own. A second call does not replace the first notification.
- Phones sign in with a redirect. If the popup closes early, sign-in continues instead of stopping. The sign-in gate does not dismiss itself while Google is still open.
