## What changed
1. Console: a background refresh no longer redraws the page. Only the parts
   whose numbers changed are swapped, so the page stays where you are reading
   (all tabs, and Launch & Capacity every 45 s).
2. Calls connect while the phone is still ringing (as Signal and WhatsApp do).
   Nothing is heard or seen on either side until Answer; Answer then starts the
   call at once. Works with phones on the older version (they connect after
   Answer, as before).
3. The answering phone no longer throws away a nearly ready connection after
   9 s and starts again. The answer goes out with "accepted" in one write.
4. The alert to the other phone leaves sooner (its push address is read while
   the microphone/camera opens).
5. Records → Call speed (console): how long real phones take at each step.
6. Listen: once the voice is on the phone, the first sentences of a written
   Broadcast are made while it is open, so Listen starts at once.
