## Visible changes
- A visitor no longer sees a creator's Circle member list or count on a
  Broadcast; they see the Broadcast's own members. The creator still sees
  their whole Circle.
- Going live no longer writes "X is live now: <title>" into Wireline chats;
  the push and the in-app notice say it (and reach the whole Circle now).
- Answering on a second phone of the same person connects after Answer
  (about 3 s), like calls before early connect. The first phone is instant.

- Push addresses (the codes that let Naluno wake a phone) are no longer on
  public profiles. The app keeps them in each person's private vault, and the
  economy worker moves the old ones there on its first runs (pushMove/state).
  Nothing changes for the person.
