# Upload — build 20261014c (calls that never connected after 14b)

Builds on what is on GitHub now (14b). Your console sign-in, the camera and
everything else are unchanged. No worker or rules change in this build.

## What was wrong
14b shortened the call by cutting every wait for the relay (TURN, the
server that carries a call through mobile networks) to 0–0.9 s. A
connection can only get the relay before its offer or answer is written.
Naluno never sends a new offer later, so a missed relay stays missed. When
the caller's relay and the answering phone's relay were **both** missing
(the caller's relay took longer than 0.9 s, and the answering phone was
told not to wait), the call could never connect on mobile data. It stayed
on "connecting" and then ended.

Two smaller 14b problems made it worse:
- Answer gave the answer already being built 0.4 s, then threw it away and
  built it again, waiting for the same camera and relay. That was about
  1 s slower, never faster.
- On an answer from a closed app, the call record was read before sign-in
  was back. If that read was refused, the answer was dropped silently.

## What changed (js/calls.js, js/pwa.js)
- Caller: waits for its relay fetch itself, up to 2.5 s, before ringing.
  This is instant when Naluno has been open (the relay is kept ready).
- Answering phone, caller has a relay: no wait at Answer (14b's speed kept).
  While it rings, it still fetches its own relay, and Answer cuts that
  short.
- Answering phone, caller has no relay: waits for its own relay fetch (only
  as long as the fetch takes; 7 s only cuts off a hang), so at least one
  side always has a relay.
- Answer waits for the answer already being built instead of restarting.
- A refused or empty early read is read again after sign-in.
- 14b's real speed-ups stay: shorter SDP wait, faster sign-in pickup, the
  call record read alongside sign-in, no 800 ms pause in MainActivity.

## Tested (here, two browsers, simulated mobile network where only relay
routes reach the other phone)
- Answer from a closed app, 5 situations × 2 runs: **14c 10/10 connect, 14b
  4/10.** Sound both ways 1.2–1.9 s after Answer (same as 14b when 14b
  connects). Worst case (both relays slow): about 4 s; on 14b, never.
- Open app, 8 situations (answer, decline, cancel, early route lost, two
  phones ×3, video with camera flip), normal and simulated mobile network:
  16/16.
- Relay service down: calls connect as fast as before. Relay service
  hanging: the ring starts after 2.5 s and the call still connects.
- 75/75 app test files, 27/27 economy worker, 12/12 call-alert worker;
  camera files unchanged.
- Not tested: real phones on real carriers. Please try a call from a phone on
  mobile data to a phone whose Naluno has been closed for over 90 minutes.

## Trade-off
If the relay service itself hangs, a caller whose relay is not yet ready waits
up to 2.5 s before the other phone rings. That is the price of the call not
failing on mobile data.
