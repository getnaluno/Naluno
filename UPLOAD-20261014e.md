worker folder, which are not on GitHub yet. Everything else is unchanged.

## Why new videos were not on the public feed
Every new Broadcast is saved as "waiting" and the economy worker lists it after
Naluno Screen has read it. On a phone, Screen's detector never finished: it
started only after a simpler check, and its 15 seconds had to cover downloading
about 14 MB (its engine and model) and reading eight frames on a slow processor.
Screen then fell back to the simple check, which sends almost every video to a
person ("Screen unsure"), even a street at night. So each new video stayed on
its maker's list ("It goes out after a first look") and never reached the
public feed unless someone pressed Let out in the console.
A second path did the same: a video switched from Private to public was sent
with no reading at all and held as "unscreened" for good.

## What changed
- The detector starts downloading when the Broadcast composer opens, runs
  alongside the simple check, and has 55 seconds (Publish already waits up to 60).
- If it still could not finish, the upload job asks it again just before saving
  (it has loaded by then); its reading replaces the simple check's. A "block"
  stays a block.
- Its findings always reach the worker (they were dropped when no still frames
  were taken).
- A video made public reuses the verdict the worker gave it before. The rules
  now stop a phone from writing a verdict itself when it creates a Broadcast.
Moderation rules are unchanged: explicit content is still stopped, unsure
content still waits for a person.
