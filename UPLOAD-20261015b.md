## What changes for people
- A new version is taken on its own, at a moment nobody notices: right after
  the app opens, when it comes back to the screen, or after a minute without a
  touch. Never during a call, an upload, a post being written, a live, a
  Broadcast or Band being open, typing, or a video playing with sound. Until
  then the Update banner shows, and a tap updates at once.
- Newest wins. A phone that missed one or more updates goes straight to the
  newest. An older copy (a slow server still handing out an old file) never
  replaces a newer one: an older worker refuses to install, and an older app
  page is never kept over a newer one.
- No reload loops while an upload is half done on GitHub: the same version is
  tried once, then offered by the banner, and taken when the upload completes.
- Control Centre: it notices a newer build and shows a bar with a Reload
  button. It never reloads by itself, because each opening asks for the
  console password. Your sign-in is unchanged.
