# Upload — build 20261015c (public Broadcasts and Signals that did not reach everyone)

Builds on what is on GitHub now (15b). Your console sign-in, the camera,
Luganda and the database rules are unchanged. No money values are involved.

## What was wrong (each one reproduced here first, on the 15b build)
Broadcasts
- A phone whose clock runs fast (and could not read the server's clock) was
  refused the whole public feed. 15b showed 0 of 60 posts.
- A phone whose clock runs slow hid every post from its last hour (by its
  clock those were "scheduled"). 15b showed 41 of 60, 1 of the 20 newest.
- "Let out" and "Restore" on the desk set listed, but not a missing
  visibility or publishAt, so an older post could be let out and still not
  appear. 15b: 0 of 2 such posts reached the feed.
- When the on-phone detector did not finish, the weak skin check decided,
  and anything in its middle band (a face, warm light, a street at night)
  was held, for trusted accounts too.

Signals
- "Everyone" was only the default after the maker had been opened once on
  that phone. "Send to Signal" from a Broadcast never went to everyone.
- The copy for everyone was written without checking: on a phone 2 hours
  fast the database refused it and nobody was told. 15b: no public copy.
- Releasing a held Signal never put it back in front of everyone.
- The public row read 120 Signals and showed 30 people: with 50 people
  posting, 15b showed 11 (1 of the 40 quieter people). A phone 10 minutes
  slow was refused the row: 0.

## What changed
Broadcasts
- The feed asks by the server's clock. The last clock difference the phone
  measured is kept, so a fast phone that cannot reach the server right now
  still asks a question the database can answer. If it is refused anyway,
  it measures again and asks further back (10 min, 1 h, 1 day, 1 week),
  and keeps trying to read the server clock while the feed is open; once it
  can, the newest posts are filled in.
- "Scheduled" is judged by the server's clock (feed and Discover).
- Let out / Restore (desk, and the worker's route) add visibility "public"
  and a publishAt in the past only when they are missing. A private post
  stays private; a scheduled post keeps its time. If the post cannot be
  read first, nothing is filled.
- The skin check on its own (detector did not finish) now holds only what
  looks like nudity or has a sexual title; its middle band goes out. Same
  rule on the phone and on the worker. When the detector runs, its verdict
  still decides, as before. The Signal maker now starts loading the
  detector when it opens, as the Broadcast maker does.
- Desk, Broadcast tab, new card "Held only by the old skin check": Check
  counts the videos held that way before today and changes nothing; Release
  writes the audit record first, then lets them out. It never touches one
  held for nudity by the detector, a sexual title, a safety hold, a private,
  taken-down or deleted post.
- The worker's legacy repair (and the desk's "Older Broadcasts missing from
  the public feed") also covers a post with no time of its own, using the
  time the document was made.

Signals
- Everyone is the default. "My connections", once chosen, is remembered.
- Who it is for is stored on both copies, held or not. Releasing a held
  Signal puts it back for everyone when that was the choice and its 24 hours
  are not over. Older held Signals with no stored choice stay with
  connections (the choice was never recorded, so it is not guessed).
- "Send to Signal" shows the same choice. It goes to everyone only when the
  Broadcast itself is public (its picture already passed that Broadcast's
  check, as a Pass-on does).
- The copy for everyone keeps its expiry inside 24 hours of the server's
  clock. If it is still refused, the phone reads the server clock and tries
  once more; if that fails, it is saved for connections and the person is
  told it did not go to everyone.
- The public row asks by the server's clock, retries a refusal, reads up to
  480 Signals in pages of 120, and has no 30-person cap.

## What I deliberately did not change
- The database rules: private, held and taken-down posts stay unreadable.
- A picture that arrives with no readable stills at all is still held
  ("unscreened"), trusted or not. Letting those out unseen would let a
  modified app post anything. They wait for the desk.
- Nothing is migrated by itself. The two repairs are buttons you press.
