# 20261015h — Wireline attachments, Signals for everyone, site motion

Unzip this onto the current repo, then open the app once so shell v331 replaces the old one.

Two deploys are required. Unzipping the files does not do either of them.

1. Deploy `signal-worker-index.js` to **naluno-broadcast-upload** before anyone sends a conversation file. Wireline calls `/w/init`, `/w/part`, `/w/complete`, and `/w/abort` on that worker. An old worker has no `/w/` routes. Do not point Wireline back at `/b/`.
2. Deploy `firestore.rules`. Without that, Signals for everyone stays empty: the list now reads `feedUntil`, and only the new rules allow that query.

Broadcast `/b/` is unchanged. Camera (`js/camera.js`) and Luganda were not edited.

## Signals for everyone

A Signal chosen for Everyone was refused as a list (the rules could not prove the old query, and a missing index refused it outright) or saved as private when the phone clock ran fast, after which it could not be made public again. A cached manifest also made the clock look days off, so the list asked from before the server's time and was refused.

The list is now one field, `feedUntil`, equal to the expiry only while the Signal is public, not held and not taken down. The phone caps the expiry so a fast clock still fits the server's 25 hours. The owner of a copy that was locked private can put it back; a desk takedown (`hidden`) cannot be undone. The clock check no longer reads the cached manifest.
