# Build 20261023a — upload

Includes 22a (Compass) and every file changed since 19d.

## What changed
**Console: the "Control Centre … is ready. Reload" bar is gone**
- It covered the desk and came back on every opening when the console
  page, or one of its files kept on the phone, was an older copy (a file
  uploaded a little after its page keeps the old copy under the new name).
- Before the password, a newer build just loads (once, never in a loop).
- After the password, a newer build waits: a small "update …" chip by the
  clock. Tap it to load now, or it loads the next time you press Lock. The
  password is still asked every time, and the way you log in is unchanged.
- Files kept on the phone are now checked with the server, so an older
  copy heals on the next opening.

**Callsign photo**
- The photo itself is the picker, on the Callsign page and in Edit
  callsign. No pencil or plus on it. Tap it and choose a picture.
- The usual picker: the whole picture is shown, the circle is clear, the
  rest is dimmed. Pinch to zoom, drag to move. The picture always fills the
  circle. Use photo saves a square of exactly what is in the circle; on the
  Callsign page it is saved straight away.

**Every button tapped**
- A new test taps every control on every tab like a finger: it must be
  reachable, do something you can see, and cause no error.
- Fixed: Toga board error ("fbDb is not defined") when it redrew before
  sign-in was set up.
- Fixed: "Enable call notifications" looked dead on a second tap when the
  browser blocks notifications; it now says so.
- Found, not changed (camera is locked): in the video-call lobby, the photo
  button at the top right cannot be tapped. The clear top bar lies over it.
  A two-line style fix is ready if you allow a change to the call screen.
