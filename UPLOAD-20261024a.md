# Build 20261024a — upload

Calls keep their camera and mic (the "flip, then black, only voice"
problem), the console opens fast again, and the audit's fixes. Includes
everything that differs from GitHub's current build (23e).

## 1. Flip the camera → picture goes black, only voice stays
**What happens:** on Android, flipping the lens hands the camera from one
lens to the other. About half a second later Naluno checks the new
picture's shape and may open the lens once more. Some phones then take the
camera away again by themselves. Nothing in the call noticed: the other
person's picture went black and **never came back**, while the sound went
on. Reproduced exactly with two simulated Android phones.

A second, worse case: when a flip could not open any lens, the fallback
opened a new camera and mic **on this phone only**. This phone looked fine;
the other person lost the picture **and the sound**.

**Fix:** while a call is up, the call checks its own camera and mic every
0.7 s. A lost camera or mic is opened again, the same lens, the same locked
way, and handed to the call. Nothing is reopened while Naluno is in the
background; a camera switched off with the button stays off; a voice call
never opens a camera; nothing is left on after hang-up. **camera.js is not
changed** (still locked).

Tested (new gate flow, 13 checks): three flips, the phone taking the camera
away, every lens refused, the mic dying, camera off, background, hang-up,
voice call. The live code fails 6 of them; this build passes all.

New measure in the console (Measures → Calls): **Camera lost mid-call** and
**Mic lost mid-call** — brought back or not. This shows how often it
happens on real phones.

**Your decision (camera lock):** two things in camera.js make the problem
more likely, and I did not touch them:
- the automatic shape check reopens the lens right after every flip
  (twice the camera hand-overs);
- a flip tap during that reopen is silently ignored.
Say the word if you want either changed (CAMERA-LOCK.md would be updated).

## 2. Console slow to open
Four delays, all removed:
1. **The worker read the password copies one after another**: up to 7
   database round trips before *every* console request (23d added two).
   Now they are read together: one round trip.
2. **A right password wrote to the database** on every unlock. Now only
   when there is something to reset.
3. **After the worker said "right", the console still hashed the password
   2–3 times and read/wrote the account copy before opening.** Now it opens
   at once and does that behind it. (Without the worker's yes, the copies
   are still checked first, exactly as before.)
4. **On a device where Naluno's service worker is not in charge yet** (first
   visit, cleared site data) every request first got a 404 from the website.
   Now it goes straight to the worker.

Simulated phone (4× slower processor), worker as it was vs as it is now:
Unlock → console **1.9 s → 0.37 s**, Unlock → data **2.5 s → 0.97 s**.
The lock-out (3 tries, 6-digit code) is unchanged and tested.
