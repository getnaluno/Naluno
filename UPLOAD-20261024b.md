# Build 20261024b — upload

Signals become Naluno's reels, and the camera flip fixes you approved.
**Includes 24a** (it was not on GitHub yet): the call keeps its camera and
mic, the console opens fast, every script stored for offline. Read
UPLOAD-20261024a.md too; its worker deploy still applies.

## 1. Signals are the reels of Naluno, in our diamond
- Each Signal is a **tall diamond** (a reel standing up): 120 × 178 on a
  phone (a little smaller on very small phones), up from 84-px diamonds.
- The clip **fills the whole diamond** (it used to sit in a small circle
  inside it). A written Signal shows its words in the middle, up to 90
  characters (it was cut at 26).
- Our colours still turn around each rim; a Signal you have seen goes
  quiet. The thread of light and its knots still join them, tip to tip.
- The name and "Short clip" sit under each diamond; how old it is sits on
  the bottom tip; a small ▶ badge low in the diamond.
- About three on screen, the rest scroll. The strip takes less than half
  the screen, so the first Broadcast is still in view.
- Taps, the viewer, New Signal and the data are unchanged. The same look
  on For You and My Broadcasts.

## 2. Camera (your go-ahead; sizes not touched)
- **A tap is never lost:** a flip tapped while the lens is still opening
  (the flip before, or Naluno's automatic reopen after a flip) now happens
  right after. Two quick taps = two flips.
- **The mic stays open during a flip.** It used to be closed and reopened
  with every flip, and again with the automatic reopen: two extra hardware
  hand-overs in the middle of a call.
- **A flip that cannot open any lens** now hands the camera and mic it
  falls back to to the call at once.
- Not touched: what is asked of the camera (sizes, shapes, lenses), how the
  picture is drawn, fitted and shown. The camera lock still holds; only the
  "how a lens is opened" fingerprint changed, recorded in CAMERA-LOCK.md.
- Left as it is on purpose: the flip does not recognise the filtered
  picture (Chrome gives it a random name). "Fixing" that would drop a chosen
  filter on every flip; today the filter code restores the right picture.
