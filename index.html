What it does

Everything you do with your people, in one place.

Most of us use one app to message, another to call, another to post, and a fourth for groups. Naluno puts them together, and asks for a name instead of your number.

Swipe to see more →

Talk privately

Send messages, voice notes and photos to people who know your Callsign. Written messages are end-to-end encrypted.

Call by name

Voice and video calls to a Callsign. No phone number and no SIM card needed.

Share what you make

Your Broadcast holds your videos, photos and writing. It stays until you delete it.

Post in one tap

Video, writing, going live, or a short clip that disappears after 24 hours.

Privacy, in plain words

Your number stays yours.

  • No phone number neededPeople reach you by your Callsign, a name you choose, not by your number.
  • Private messages stay privateWritten messages are end-to-end encrypted, so only you and the person you're writing to can read them.
  • Rooms that clean upA Band is deleted for good two hours after the last person leaves.
  • No outside ad networksNaluno does not use outside ad networks to follow you around.

Who makes Naluno

Built by a writer.

Naluno is made by E J Magambo, a novelist with four books published on Amazon.

It started from a writer's problem: having somewhere to share your work and talk with the people around it, without handing over a phone number or getting lost in a crowd.
