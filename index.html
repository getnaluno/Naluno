Six ways to be reached

Naluno borrows its language from radio, because that is genuinely how it works — you tune to the people you want, and the rest stays quiet.

88.1callsign

Your name on the air

One handle, yours. People find you by it, reach you by it, and nothing else about you is public unless you decide it is.

94.6frequencies

The people you chose

Not followers, not suggestions, not an algorithm's guess. A list you built deliberately, and can prune whenever it stops being true.

101.3wireline

Messages, voice notes, photos, documents

Direct conversation with the people on your Frequencies. Send a feeling when words are too much work — the mood cards say it without saying it.

107.8signal · 24h

Short clips that don't outlive the moment

Post something small, let it go. Signals expire on their own, so nothing accumulates into a record you didn't ask for.

114.2broadcast

The things worth keeping

Longer video that stays put, with a Circle of people around it, questions, results, and a conversation attached to the thing itself. Go live when it matters.

120.5band

Rooms with your people in them

Group conversation without the group-chat sprawl. Compass and Spark for the two hardest problems — finding someone nearby, and swapping details in person without typing a thing.
