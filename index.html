What it is

Naluno is a communication app, not a feed. You choose who is on your Frequencies. Nothing here is suggested to you.

88.1 · Callsign

Your name on the air

Pick a handle and a password. Google still works if you have it. You are reachable without handing anyone a SIM.

101.3 · Wireline

A private line

Messages, voice, photos, documents. Text is end-to-end encrypted. Call from the same place. Spark swaps handles in person — no typing.

114.2 · Band · Broadcast

In the moment, or on purpose

Band is a room that does not keep the talk. Broadcast is video you leave behind. Signal is a short clip that fades on its own.
