What Naluno is

Communication, creators and communities — one place

Most of the internet splits those into different apps. A private line here. A creator feed there. A group somewhere else. Naluno is being built so they do not have to live apart.

You pick a Callsign — a name, not a phone number. People who know it can write, call, and find your Broadcasts. Around that sit Wireline, Band, Broadcast, Signals and Compass. The opportunity is a layer that creators and communities can actually grow around, without an outside ad network in the middle, and without turning a handle into a public billboard.

How it fits together

The shape of the product

Callsign is the person. Wireline and calls are the private line. Band is the room. Broadcast and Signals are what creators leave. Compass sits beside all of it.

Participation is counted so the ecosystem can grow around it — not as a public billboard, and not as a promise of money.

Current stage

Being prepared for a wider launch

Naluno is in active development. The core product is already in people’s hands. The work now is to harden it, grow the creator side, and prepare a public launch that can take the load. This page exists so that conversation can start before a formal raise is on paper.

How it can sustain itself

Direction, not a forecast

Naluno is meant to grow around the activity on it. Labelled first-party ads — never an outside network, never an auction. Creator Support later, when a payments company is connected. That is the direction. It is not live money, and it is not a projection.
