Where Naluno is today

A working product, in early access.

Everything on the home page works now: private messages, voice and video calls, Bands, Broadcasts, short clips and the Compass guide, in the browser and on Android.

Naluno has a small first group of members and is growing. We share the numbers, and the plan for the next twelve months, in conversation.

Why now

More people are coming online, and they live on their phones.

How it can pay for itself

A direction, not a forecast.

Naluno is meant to grow with the activity on it: clearly labelled ads from Naluno itself, never an outside ad network, and support for creators from their audiences once a payments partner is in place.

None of this is a promise of income or a projection.
