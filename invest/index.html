About Naluno

A private line in a loud square

Most apps ask for a phone number, then spend the relationship broadcasting to everyone. Naluno inverts that. You pick a Callsign — a name, not a SIM. People who know it can write and call you. Everyone else cannot.

Around that private line sit the rest of the product: Wireline (messages that live on the phone), Band (rooms that wipe), Broadcast and Signal (video you leave, and clips that fade), Spark (find someone standing next to you). The opportunity is a communications layer that creators and communities can actually own — without an ad network in the middle, and without turning a handle into a public billboard.

What has been built

Infrastructure, not a slide
