# Luganda masters

Played as recorded. Speed, pitch and the words are not changed.

- `male.mp3` — Magambo, male. One continuous take (no gaps between words).
- `female.wav` — female. One continuous take.

Listen can play a saved line only when the words match that line. These takes are not separate words, so they are not cut up and they are not sped up.

To let Listen use a master, write its exact words in `masters.json` (the `text` of that file). Until then the console plays them, and Listen uses the recordings saved in the console's Luganda tab, then Naluno's own Luganda voice for anything not recorded.
